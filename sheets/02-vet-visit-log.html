How to Use · Vet-Visit Log

An eight-row visit history, seven vaccine-due rows, zero panic in December.

What this sheet builds

A run-down of what happened at the clinic, and — more usefully — a due-date table that turns future bookings from memory work into calendar work. Booster notices get lost; a written row doesn’t.

Sixty-second setup

  1. Backfill from your last clinic paperwork: date, reason, anything due next.
  2. Missing details live at the clinic — one short phone call, pet’s name in hand, is usually all it takes.
  3. Each visit, fill its row while you wait for the receipt. Memory is a leaky bucket here.

Vaccine-due rows, done kindly

  • Write the next-due date in the biggest words you own — this column is the whole point of the sheet.
  • The “call and book by” column is your cue, a week or two ahead of the due date. Book by that, and you’re never chasing an expired vaccine.
  • Pair each row with the pet profile sheet’s allergy and meds boxes — the two sheets answer each other.

A quiet tip

Tape the sheet inside the folder’s front cover and take visible dates out loud to family once a season — shared knowledge means the appointment doesn’t always fall on the same tired person.
