How to Use · Lost-Pet Flyer

The flier nobody ever wants to print — which is exactly why it’s best printed early.

What this sheet builds

Preparedness with a soft landing: a flyer that only needs a photo, a name and a phone when it matters. Filling one before anything happens takes minutes; filling one at midday on the worst Thursday tends to take tears.

Sixty-second setup

  1. Pencil in the clinic’s phone today — it doesn’t change often, and it’s the one number every stranger has in common.
  2. Leave the rest blank until needed, then fill with the thickest pen in the house.
  3. Reprint from the folder as needed; keep the master clean of coffee rings.

If it happens for real

  • Walk, don’t drive, the routes the pet already knows; call while walking.
  • Say “lost pet, please don’t chase” out loud — it gives neighbours the one instruction that helps.
  • Kind people help; anyone asking a fee or a code to return the pet is resisted — work with your vet and the local shelter instead.

Warm tip

The “quirks” line matters more than it looks: a pet that bolts at scooters or hides under porches tells a finder exactly how to help without adding fear.
