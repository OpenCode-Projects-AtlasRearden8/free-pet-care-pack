Pet Care, Pen & Paper

One friendly folder for every animal in the house — printed once, filled in slowly, ready before it’s needed.

Hello, fellow animal person. This little pack is the paperwork half of loving a pet: who they are, when their shots come due, who feeds them, and where to look first if they ever worry you. It’s all pen-and-paper on purpose — no accounts, no apps, nothing to remember a password for.

What’s inside

Pet profile sheetOne page per animal: looks, quirks, routines and vet basics at a glance.
Vet-visit logEvery visit in one place, plus vaccine-due rows so boosters never sneak up.
Sitter handoff cardEverything a pet sitter needs, on two cut-out cards — one to give, one for the fridge.
Feeding schedule gridA week of portions and tick-boxes; hang it wherever the bowls live.
Lost-pet flyerPhoto box, big name, tear-off tabs — filled in before anyone hopes it’s never needed.
Pet-safe room checklistWalk the house at nose height and tick what’s snug, room by room.

Set up in ten minutes

Grab a folder, a binder or a drawer that gets opened anyway. Paper near the treats lives longer than paper in a drawer of doom.
Print one pet profile per animal — copies are free, and animals happily refuse to share a page.
Fill the vet log’s vaccine rows from your last clinic paperwork or tag; if a date’s missing, the clinic can fill it in by pet’s name — they welcome the call.
Hang the feeding grid wherever the bowls live, and stick the second handoff card to the fridge. You’re done properly.

Kind notes

It all stays at home — and a promise

The sheets are plain HTML/CSS turned into PDFs on your own machine: no sign-ups, no internet, and filled-in pages live on paper where a busy morning can find them.

Print at “actual size”; every sheet works in black & white. US Letter? Rebuild with build.py --paper letter. Rules of thumb here are general — your vet’s advice about your animals always wins.
