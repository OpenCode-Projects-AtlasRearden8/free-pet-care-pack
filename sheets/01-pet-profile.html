How to Use · Pet Profile Sheet

One sheet per animal, kept where the food comes from.

What this sheet builds

A dossier the whole family can read in one glance — and that a stranger, a sitter or an emergency clinic could use if you’re not around. The photo matters more than perfect handwriting: faces get animals found and remembered.

Sixty-second setup

  1. Tape in a photo, then fill the top row from memory — nobody’s checking your spelling of the breed.
  2. Copy the vet line straight off your last clinic receipt, and the chip line off the adoption or purchase papers.
  3. Leave what you don’t know blank. Blanks get fixed on the next vet visit; guesses never do.

Keep it true

  • Weights and meds drift: re-copy the sheet whenever the collar feels loose, or with the seasons.
  • Old copies stay in the folder — a stack of honest old pages beats one polished page of stale facts.
  • Teens and tweens can own a section: quirks and loves are theirs to write, and they notice everything.

More than one animal?

One sheet each, newest on top. The “worries” box is the hidden gem: writing down what startles each animal makes thunder seasons and vet days dramatically calmer.
