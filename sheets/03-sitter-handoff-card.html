How to Use · Sitter Handoff Card

Two cards per sheet: the upper one goes with the sitter, the lower one takes the fridge’s best spot.

What this sheet builds

A pocket-sized brief a sitter can digest in the doorway: times, tricks, who to call and where the leash lives. It also quietly doubles as your own emergency card — fill it for yourself once and hand it over when a flat tyre eats your evening.

Sixty-second setup

  1. Draft the boxes by hand first; the sitter card wants your normal words, not tidy handwriting printouts.
  2. Write the “nevers” in capital letters — sitters scan, and capitals get read.
  3. Attach the pet photo on the back so the card belongs to the right animal even in a multi-pet house.

The fridge copy is not spare

  • The fridge card keeps your family honest about the meds schedule when you’re home, too.
  • Refresh both cards whenever anything changes — new meds, new clinic, new trick for pill time.
  • Overnight stays? Add what’s forbidden in bed, and which toy negotiates sleep.

Warm tip

Sitters photograph the fridge card as their own backup — that’s the point of it living where things get read. A photo a day on the foot check-box keeps worried owners calm and the pet a touch famous.
